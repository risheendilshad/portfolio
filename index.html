About Me.

Hi, I’m Risheen Dilshad, a passionate web designer and developer based in India.

I’m an MCA postgraduate with hands-on experience in web development and a background as a Front-End Developer. I currently work as a freelance website developer, helping businesses build modern, responsive, and user-friendly websites that look great across all devices.

I enjoy turning ideas into clean, engaging digital experiences. With a strong foundation in HTML, CSS, JavaScript and modern web technologies, I focus on creating websites that are visually appealing, functional, responsive, and easy to use.

I’m a creative thinker, flexible, and easy to communicate with. I enjoy understanding a client's ideas and turning them into websites they can be proud of.

During my academic journey, I also participated in web designing competitions and won prizes, which strengthened my creativity, problem-solving skills, and passion for web design.

Let’s turn your idea into a modern web experience.
