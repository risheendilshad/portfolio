About Me.

I'm Risheen, a front-end web developer and freelance web designer with a Master's in Computer Applications (MCA). I don't believe in one-size-fits-all websites — every project I take on is designed from scratch to reflect the unique identity of the person or business behind it. My focus is on creating fully customizable designs that adapt to your specific goals, brand, and audience, not templated layouts that look like everyone else's. Whether it's a distinctive color palette, a custom layout, or interactive elements built just for your site, I make sure the final product feels authentically yours. With a strong foundation in front-end development, I bring together clean code and creative design to build websites that are as functional as they are original. As a freelancer, I work closely with each client to understand their vision and translate it into a site that stands out, not blends in. When I'm not designing, I'm exploring new tools and techniques to keep pushing what's possible with custom web design.
